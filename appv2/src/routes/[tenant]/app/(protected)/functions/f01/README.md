# F01 Strategy & Enterprise Planning

This route tree is the active V2 implementation of F01 Strategy & Enterprise Planning.

Implemented V2 workspaces currently cover F01.01 direction, F01.02 environmental analysis, F01.03 strategic planning, F01.04 business planning, F01.06 goal/KPI management and F01.07 strategic review. F01.05 Operating Model and F01.08 Scenario & Foresight remain explicit V2 delivery gaps and must not be presented as available workflows until their transaction surfaces exist.

F01 uses one strategy-cycle context, server-side effective permissions, transactional audit/outbox evidence, controlled CRUD/lifecycle policies and governed major/minor versions. Association editing is part of record governance: lineage must be amendable while mutable, validated against the parent context and frozen once the relevant lifecycle/version is published.

The governing product and domain architecture is `docs/world-class/15-f01-strategy-enterprise-planning-v2.md`.
