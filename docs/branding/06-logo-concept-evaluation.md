# NuBlox Logo Concept Evaluation

**Document status:** Draft v1.0  
**Date:** 2026-08-17  
**Scope:** Evaluation and first-round selection of NuBlox visual identity concepts  
**Parent documents:** `docs/branding/04-visual-identity-brief.md`, `docs/branding/05-logo-concept-directions.md`

## 1. Purpose

This document evaluates the first visible NuBlox logo concept round against the strategic and functional requirements defined in the visual identity brief.

The objective is to select a direction for refinement rather than approve a finished logo.

The first round explored four territories:

1. **Modular System** — a modular `N` constructed from connected geometric units;
2. **Connected Structure** — an interlocking abstract structure expressing connection and integration;
3. **Constructed Monogram** — a compact geometric monogram centred on the `N`/`NB` territory; and
4. **Wordmark-led Identity** — a strong NuBlox wordmark with restrained proprietary detail.

The visual concept board is exploratory artwork. Final identity assets must be rebuilt as controlled vector geometry and must not be treated as production-ready logo files.

## 2. Evaluation method

Each direction is scored from **1 to 5** against the criteria defined in `05-logo-concept-directions.md`.

The scoring considers the visible first-round execution, not only the theoretical concept territory.

| Criterion | Modular System | Connected Structure | Constructed Monogram | Wordmark-led |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Strategic fit | 5 | 4 | 4 | 4 |
| Distinctiveness | 4 | 3 | 3 | 4 |
| Name fit | 5 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| Enterprise credibility | 4 | 4 | 4 | 5 |
| Digital scalability | 5 | 4 | 5 | 4 |
| Wordmark legibility | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 |
| Monochrome performance | 4 | 4 | 5 | 5 |
| Product-system fit | 5 | 4 | 5 | 5 |
| UI compatibility | 5 | 4 | 5 | 5 |
| International longevity | 4 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| Reproducibility | 4 | 4 | 5 | 5 |
| Ownability potential | 4 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| **Total / 60** | **54** | **44** | **52** | **56** |

The raw score does not by itself determine the selection. The brand's central strategic idea — modular, connected operating capability — receives additional qualitative weight.

## 3. Direction 1 — Modular System

### Assessment

The Modular System is the strongest expression of the NuBlox product and business proposition.

The visible concept creates a recognisable `N` from connected geometric components. This links naturally to:

- the `NuBlox` name;
- modular product architecture;
- shared platform foundations;
- connected capabilities;
- extensibility; and
- digital systems thinking.

It also produces a strong square application-icon silhouette, which is important for the product UI, favicon, PWA and future mobile contexts.

### Strengths

- immediately related to the NuBlox name;
- clear at small and large sizes;
- naturally supports a branded-house system;
- capable of influencing motion, diagrams and product-system graphics;
- avoids literal construction imagery;
- strong relationship between symbol and wordmark;
- credible in enterprise software contexts.

### Weaknesses to resolve

- the current gradient treatment is too dependent on colour and should not define the mark;
- individual block segmentation should be simplified for very small sizes;
- the geometry must become more proprietary so it does not resemble a generic modular `N`;
- the mark should work perfectly in one flat colour before any richer colour treatment is considered.

### Decision

**Advance to refinement.**

This is the preferred strategic direction.

## 4. Direction 2 — Connected Structure

### Assessment

The Connected Structure communicates integration effectively, but the current execution moves too close to familiar technology symbolism.

The interlocking hexagonal/chain-like form could be interpreted as:

- blockchain;
- networking;
- cybersecurity;
- integration middleware; or
- a generic technology platform.

Its relationship to the name `NuBlox` is weaker than the other directions.

### Strengths

- clearly communicates connection;
- visually balanced;
- works well as an app icon;
- offers motion potential.

### Weaknesses

- generic connected-technology territory;
- weaker brand-name association;
- lower ownability potential;
- possible confusion with existing software-sector marks;
- less direct expression of modular platform architecture.

### Decision

**Do not advance in its current form.**

Useful ideas around connection may still inform the wider graphic system, but the symbol itself should not be the primary logo direction.

## 5. Direction 3 — Constructed Monogram

### Assessment

The monogram territory remains strategically valid, but the visible first-round execution reads primarily as a stylised `N` rather than a distinctive `NB` construction.

This creates a strong compact mark but does not yet deliver enough proprietary NuBlox ownership.

### Strengths

- excellent small-size potential;
- simple silhouette;
- strong monochrome performance;
- suitable for software UI and application icons;
- independent of any single industry.

### Weaknesses

- currently resembles the broad family of geometric `N` technology logos;
- the `B` idea is insufficiently present;
- weaker direct relationship to the modular proposition than Direction 1;
- ownability would depend on significant geometric refinement.

### Decision

**Retain as a reserve/refinement influence, but do not lead the next round.**

The best idea from this direction is the emphasis on a compact, simple, proprietary silhouette. That principle should be applied to the Modular System refinement.

## 6. Direction 4 — Wordmark-led Identity

### Assessment

The Wordmark-led direction performs extremely well as a mature B2B software identity.

It is clear, scalable, professional and flexible. It also avoids over-explaining the brand through symbolism.

However, the visible concept is not yet distinctive enough to carry the whole identity without a stronger proprietary typographic feature or companion symbol.

### Strengths

- excellent readability;
- strong enterprise credibility;
- strong monochrome performance;
- easy product endorsement system;
- broad international and sector longevity;
- low visual clutter.

### Weaknesses

- weaker standalone app-icon solution;
- current custom detail is subtle enough to risk genericness;
- does not express the platform proposition as strongly as the Modular System;
- would require custom letterform development to become meaningfully ownable.

### Decision

**Advance as the secondary direction and wordmark reference.**

The wordmark discipline from this route should be combined with the preferred modular symbol rather than discarded.

## 7. First-round selection

The recommended direction is a **hybrid refinement strategy** rather than a literal adoption of one generated concept.

### Primary direction

**Modular System symbol**

Use the modular `N` idea as the foundation for the NuBlox symbol.

### Wordmark direction

**Wordmark-led typography**

Use the clarity, maturity and restraint of Direction 4 to develop the final `NuBlox` wordmark.

### Supporting influence

**Constructed Monogram simplicity**

Use Direction 3's compactness and monochrome discipline as a test for whether the modular symbol has been simplified enough.

### Rejected primary route

**Connected Structure**

Do not proceed with the chain/hexagonal connected symbol as the master logo.

## 8. Selected identity hypothesis

The next round should test the following hypothesis:

> **A proprietary modular `N` symbol, paired with a precise custom NuBlox wordmark, can express connected operating capability while remaining simple enough for serious enterprise software and small digital applications.**

This is now the preferred NuBlox identity direction.

## 9. Refinement requirements

The next design round should produce at least three substantially different refinements of the Modular System direction.

Each refinement should test a different structural approach while retaining the same strategy.

### Refinement A — Continuous Modular N

Create the `N` from a minimal number of joined geometric components with as few internal breaks as possible.

Objective: maximum small-size clarity.

### Refinement B — Interlocking Modular N

Use two or three clearly interacting units that visibly connect to form the `N`.

Objective: stronger expression of integration and shared capability.

### Refinement C — Negative-space Modular N

Use a compact outer structure in which the `N` emerges partly through negative space.

Objective: greater proprietary ownership and stronger monochrome silhouette.

## 10. Wordmark refinement requirements

The NuBlox wordmark should be developed alongside the symbol rather than selected independently.

Areas to test:

- custom `N` relationship to the symbol;
- capital `B` geometry;
- `Blox` rhythm and spacing;
- distinctive but restrained `x` terminal;
- optical relationship between `Nu` and `Blox`;
- symbol-to-wordmark spacing;
- horizontal and compact lockups.

The spelling must remain immediately readable as **NuBlox**.

## 11. Colour approach for the next round

The next geometry round should still be judged first in monochrome.

Only after a geometry shortlist exists should colour be used for comparative testing.

The current blue/teal concept palette is useful as an exploratory direction but is **not yet an approved NuBlox colour system**.

Colour testing should compare:

1. deep technical blue;
2. blue/indigo;
3. blue/teal relationship; and
4. neutral-led treatment with one proprietary accent.

## 12. Production guardrails

No generated concept image should be used directly as the final logo asset.

Before final approval, the selected mark must be:

- reconstructed as precise vector geometry;
- tested at favicon and app-icon sizes;
- tested in pure black and pure white;
- tested on light and dark UI surfaces;
- evaluated for optical balance;
- evaluated in product lockups;
- checked for visual similarity against relevant existing brands;
- subject to trademark/legal clearance where appropriate; and
- exported through a controlled SVG asset pipeline.

## 13. Current decision

**Preferred identity direction:** Modular System + precision wordmark.

**Status:** Selected for second-round refinement; not final brand approval.

The next branding deliverable should be `07-selected-identity-direction.md`, containing the refined symbol geometry strategy, wordmark construction principles, initial colour shortlist and practical application tests.
