# NuBlox Business Entity

**Document status:** Draft v1.0  
**Date:** 2026-08-15  
**Scope:** Corporate/business identity foundation for NuBlox

## 1. Purpose of this document

This document defines the strategic business identity of NuBlox before the detailed product brand, visual identity, messaging system and marketing materials are developed.

It distinguishes the **business entity**, the **master commercial brand**, and the **software platform** so that future products and services can grow under a coherent structure.

This is a strategic business-definition document. It does not assert that a particular legal entity name has been incorporated or registered. Legal-company naming and registration should be confirmed separately.

## 2. Business identity

### Working corporate name

**NuBlox Digital Applications**

### Master commercial brand

**NuBlox**

### Business category

Software and digital technology.

### Primary commercial model

Software as a Service (SaaS), supported where appropriate by implementation, integration, migration, training, consultancy, API and other platform-related services.

### Initial vertical market

Construction and the built environment.

### Initial geographic market

United Kingdom, with the platform designed for future internationalisation and regionalisation.

## 3. Business definition

NuBlox is a digital technology business that designs, develops and operates integrated software applications for businesses and professionals, with an initial focus on construction and the built environment.

NuBlox is not defined solely as a construction-software product. The business is intended to create and operate an extensible ecosystem of digital applications that can support industry-specific and general business operations through shared data, shared workflows and common platform services.

## 4. Purpose

**To simplify how businesses operate by replacing fragmented processes, disconnected software and repetitive administration with connected digital applications.**

## 5. Vision

**To create a connected digital environment in which businesses can manage their people, projects, processes, information and commercial operations through intelligent, integrated applications.**

## 6. Mission

**To design practical, integrated digital applications that reduce complexity, improve productivity and give businesses better control over their operations.**

## 7. The problem NuBlox exists to solve

Many businesses rely on a fragmented collection of systems and manual processes, including:

- accounting systems;
- CRM applications;
- spreadsheets;
- email;
- messaging applications;
- document repositories;
- estimating applications;
- project-management software;
- timesheets;
- HR systems;
- procurement systems;
- compliance systems;
- asset-management systems;
- reporting tools; and
- industry-specific applications.

This fragmentation commonly creates:

- duplicate data entry;
- duplicated administration;
- disconnected workflows;
- inconsistent records;
- poor operational visibility;
- information silos;
- avoidable errors and delays;
- weak auditability; and
- increased operating cost.

NuBlox addresses this by combining shared structured data, reusable business capabilities, industry-specific workflows and controlled collaboration in one extensible platform.

## 8. Corporate positioning

NuBlox should be positioned as:

> **A vertical business-application company building deeply integrated operating software for real-world industries.**

For the initial market, this becomes a platform capable of serving the wider built-environment ecosystem rather than a single occupation or type of contractor.

## 9. Initial customer ecosystem

The initial NuBlox market includes, but is not limited to:

- clients and asset owners;
- property developers;
- architects;
- engineers;
- consultants;
- quantity surveyors;
- main contractors;
- subcontractors;
- specialist contractors;
- suppliers;
- manufacturers;
- plant and equipment operators;
- facilities managers;
- tradespeople;
- self-employed professionals;
- professional practices;
- SMEs; and
- enterprise organisations.

The strategic intent is to support relationships and workflows across the supply chain rather than optimise only for a single organisation type.

## 10. Core value proposition

NuBlox provides businesses and professionals with a connected operating environment for managing work that would otherwise be distributed across separate systems, spreadsheets, messages and manual processes.

The platform should combine:

- business-management capabilities;
- project and operational capabilities;
- profession-specific functionality;
- shared structured records;
- controlled cross-organisation collaboration;
- workflow and automation;
- reporting and intelligence; and
- extensible integrations.

## 11. Core business principles

### Practicality

Technology must solve a genuine operational problem and improve how work is performed.

### Simplicity

Complex industries should not be burdened by unnecessarily complicated software.

### Connection

People, information, processes and applications should work together rather than exist in isolated silos.

### Accuracy

Business-critical information must be structured, trustworthy, traceable and auditable.

### Ownership

Customers should retain meaningful control over their organisation, workflows, permissions and information.

### Progress

NuBlox should improve working practices rather than merely reproduce paper-based or legacy processes in digital form.

## 12. Product philosophy

NuBlox should model how organisations and professionals actually operate.

The system should therefore be based on reusable capabilities, roles, permissions, workflows and shared records rather than rigid assumptions based solely on job titles.

This aligns with the governing product rule already established for the platform:

> **NuBlox models what people and organisations do, not only what their job title is.**

## 13. What NuBlox should not become

NuBlox should not be reduced to:

- a generic project-management application;
- an accounting package;
- an estimating-only application;
- a collection of unrelated SaaS modules;
- software designed only for major contractors;
- a system that requires every participant in a supply chain to operate identically; or
- a digital recreation of inefficient paper processes.

NuBlox should instead provide a coherent operating platform in which specialist functions share common data, platform services and workflow foundations.

## 15. Revenue model

### Primary revenue

- SaaS subscriptions;
- organisation subscriptions;
- user licensing where appropriate;
- product or capability subscriptions;
- usage-based platform services; and
- enterprise agreements.

### Potential supporting revenue

- implementation services;
- data migration;
- system integration;
- training;
- consultancy;
- API or high-volume platform usage;
- premium automation and intelligence capabilities;
- specialist industry applications;
- marketplace or partner services; and
- transaction-based services where commercially and legally appropriate.

These supporting models are options for future business development and are not commitments to initial product scope.

## 16. Strategic differentiation

NuBlox should differentiate through the combination of:

1. **Industry depth** — functionality designed around real built-environment work.
2. **Shared platform architecture** — specialist functions operate on common business data and services.
3. **Cross-functional integration** — commercial, operational, project and administrative workflows are connected.
4. **Flexible capability modelling** — users are enabled according to what they do, not restricted to a single title.
5. **Cross-organisation working** — controlled collaboration across clients, consultants, contractors, suppliers and other parties.
6. **Structured information** — operational records become reusable business data rather than isolated documents.
7. **Extensibility** — integrations, APIs and future applications can expand the ecosystem without fragmenting the core platform.

## 17. Corporate characteristics

NuBlox should consistently present itself as:

- capable;
- modern;
- practical;
- precise;
- dependable;
- connected;
- technically credible; and
- progressive.

It should avoid appearing gimmicky, overly playful, needlessly complex or dependent on construction-industry visual clichés.
