# Domain Guide: Finance

This guide covers finance operations from invoicing through accounting close.

## A. Finance workspace entry

1. Open Finance from primary navigation.
2. Confirm finance business workspace links are visible.
3. Select required finance sub-workspace.

![Figure 08 - Finance Workspace](screenshots/08-finance-workspace.png)

## B. Invoices and Billing

1. Open Invoices to create or manage invoice records.
2. Open Billing to prepare billing lines and issue actions.
3. Confirm document/value/status updates.

![Figure 27 - Finance Invoices](screenshots/27-finance-invoices.png)
![Figure 36 - Finance Billing](screenshots/36-finance-billing.png)

## C. Payments and Receivables

1. Open Payments to record and allocate payments.
2. Open Receivables to review outstanding balances.
3. Reconcile item status after allocation.

![Figure 28 - Finance Payments](screenshots/28-finance-payments.png)
![Figure 29 - Finance Receivables](screenshots/29-finance-receivables.png)

## D. Collections and Credit

1. Open Collections to action collection workflows.
2. Open Collections Automation for policy-driven actions.
3. Open Credit Control for limits and holds.

![Figure 30 - Finance Collections](screenshots/30-finance-collections.png)
![Figure 31 - Collections Automation](screenshots/31-finance-collections-automation.png)
![Figure 32 - Credit Control](screenshots/32-finance-credit-control.png)

## E. Bad Debt and Tax

1. Open Bad Debt to process write-off/recovery controls.
2. Open Tax to review tax records.
3. Open Tax Relief to process relief workflows.

![Figure 33 - Bad Debt](screenshots/33-finance-bad-debt.png)
![Figure 34 - Tax](screenshots/34-finance-tax.png)
![Figure 35 - Tax Relief](screenshots/35-finance-tax-relief.png)

## F. Accounting, Periods, Reports, Year-End

1. Open Accounting for posting/control activities.
2. Open Accounting Periods to manage open/close lifecycle.
3. Open Accounting Reports to run reporting outputs.
4. Open Year-End to execute close controls.
