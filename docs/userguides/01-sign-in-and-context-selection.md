# Guide 01: Sign-In and Context Selection

## Purpose

Use this guide to authenticate, choose your organisation context, and land in the correct workspace shell.

## Preconditions

- You have an active user account.
- You know your sign-in email and password.
- You are a member of at least one organisation.

## Procedure

1. Open the sign-in route.
2. Enter your email in the **Email** field.
3. Enter your password in the **Password** field.
4. Select **Sign in**.
5. On the organisation selection screen, choose the organisation you want to work in.
6. Confirm you are redirected to the NuBlox dashboard.

## Success criteria

- You arrive at `/dashboard` after selecting an organisation.
- Top-level navigation and workspace tools are visible.

## Troubleshooting

- If you see `Invalid email or password`, verify credentials and retry.
- If no organisations are listed, request membership assignment from your administrator.
